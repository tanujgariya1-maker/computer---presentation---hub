01 · Introduction

Why use movement?

🎯

Focus attention

Reveal important information at the right moment instead of showing everything at once.

✨

Improve engagement

Simple effects can make a presentation feel dynamic and easier to follow.

🧭

Guide the audience

Use motion to show sequence, relationships, and the flow of an idea.

⚖️

Use effects wisely

Too many effects can distract from your message. Keep movement purposeful.

02 · Animation

What is an Animation?

An animation is an effect applied to an object inside a slide—such as text, a picture, shape, chart, or icon.

Entrance

Brings an object onto the slide.

AppearFadeFly In

Emphasis

Draws attention to an object already visible.

PulseSpinGrow/Shrink

Exit

Removes an object from the slide.

DisappearFadeFly Out

Motion Path

Moves an object along a chosen path.

LinesArcsCustom Path
03 · Transition

What is a Transition?

A transition is the visual effect that happens when you move from one slide to another.

Fade

Smooth and professional for most topics.

Push

Moves the new slide into view.

Wipe

Reveals the next slide in a direction.

Morph

Creates a smooth transformation between matching objects on slides.

04 · Compare

Animation vs. Transition

🎬 Animation

Works on: objects within a slide.

Example: A title fades in or a diagram moves.

🖥️ Transition

Works on: the change between slides.

Example: Slide 2 fades in after Slide 1.

06 · Best practices

Tips for a professional presentation

Keep it consistent

Use a small set of effects instead of a different animation on every object.

Keep it short

Long effects slow down the presentation. Use timing that supports your speaking pace.

Animate with purpose

Ask: “Does this effect help the audience understand something?”

Practice

Run the presentation before presenting so timing feels natural.

07 · Conclusion

Move the audience, not just the objects.

Animations control how objects appear and behave. Transitions control how slides change. Used thoughtfully, both can make your PowerPoint clearer, smoother, and more memorable.
